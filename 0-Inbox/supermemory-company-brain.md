# How to Build Your Company Brain (in 5 mins)

> Captured from @supermemory's thread. Every company will have a company brain, whether they believe it or not. This is the bet that I'm making, and I'm constantly seeing very future forward startups come to us for their own setup.

## What is a company brain?

Usually, there's:
- A **knowledge base**
- An **agent harness** on top of the knowledge base

Sounds simple! Right? The challenges start when you encounter the constraints of companies:
- Where does the knowledge come from?
- How do you manage permissions?
- How do you give this to the agent in the best way?
- How do you manage correctness over time, updates in information, and how do you remove the unnecessary stuff as time continues?

For these things, a lot of companies have turned to @supermemory to be the "context" provider for their company brain. But even then, having the right context isn't the final challenge. There's challenges in proactiveness, creating automations, etc. It practically becomes a full time job.

## How to do it

There's two ways:
1. Use a **managed solution**
2. Truly **build your own**

Supermemory is the right fit for both. Today, the thread covers the **managed solution**.

## Why supermemory?

- Started from the **memory layer**, thinking about context for long-running agents for months.
- Built into the **Hermes agent harness** too.
- Can carry knowledge **outside of supermemory** into your own tools.
- **Feature-rich**: API, MCP, CLI, etc.
- Use it with **any model of your choice** — no extra charge for models.
- Use it **outside of Slack** — knowledge is not constrained.

> A super employee with all the knowledge of your company, lives in your slack. — Learns how the team works and talks. — Works across all your tools. — Permission controls built-in for big teams. 2 mins to set up. just invite to slack

## The 5 steps

### Step 1: Invite it to your company
- Click [https://supermemory.link/brain](https://supermemory.link/brain)
- It opens Slack and asks to be invited to public channels
- If invited to public channels, it automatically learns from them and can look back **3 months**
- In the background, supermemory collects context and learns about you

### Step 2: Connect your tools
- Head to [https://app.supermemory.ai/?view=configure](https://app.supermemory.ai/?view=configure) and connect as many tools as you'd need

### Step 3: Talk to the bot as you would
- It can literally do everything: sales, engineering, research, review, even tagging other bots to code things
- Customer research and sales help
- Answers questions about HR and office
- *"Quite literally, it can do everything that you would expect from a senior employee"*

### Step 4: Use it outside of Slack
- Knowledge is useful even outside Slack
- Plugins with **Claude Code**, **Codex**, etc.
- Follow instructions → done
- These show up on the **"Agents" tab** of your supermemory account

### Step 5: Create automations and tasks
- E.g. *"Every morning give me a daily brief of the urgent issues to be working on right now"*
- Works in DMs, channels, or anywhere you'd want

## The pitch

> Congrats! You now have a full company brain for yourself. Enjoy the benefits of an ever growing all knowing agent that has all the tools and knowledge of your company :)
