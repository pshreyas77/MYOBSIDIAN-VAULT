## Bottom Line

- **Raw performance & multi-core:** i5-10500H > i5-8350U by ~1.7-2.2x multi, ~1.3-1.5x single sustained
- **Efficiency & thermals:** i5-8350U wins on power efficiency, battery life, cooler operation
- **For Data Analyst / AI-ML workloads:** i5-10500H is stronger for sustained CPU tasks, compiling, data transforms. i5-8350U is better for portability.
