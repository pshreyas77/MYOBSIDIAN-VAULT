## Summary
Research confirms that rain-induced waterlogging and flooding—specifically cases where roads are blocked and water enters homes—is a **pan-India problem**. As of 2024-2026, this issue has been documented in **all 28 states** and **all 8 Union Territories** of India.

Four regional patterns identified:
1. **Urban Crisis (Drainage Failure):** Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune, Kochi — chronic storm water drain failure
2. **River & Plain States (Overflow):** Bihar, UP, Assam, Odisha — flat topography, prolonged stagnation
3. **Mountain States (Flash Floods):** Himachal, Uttarakhand, Sikkim, NE states — cloudbursts, landslides
4. **Dry/Arid States (Sudden Inundation):** Rajasthan, Haryana, Punjab — poor soil absorption

All 8 UTs affected including Delhi NCR, J&K, Ladakh, Chandigarh, Puducherry, and island territories.
