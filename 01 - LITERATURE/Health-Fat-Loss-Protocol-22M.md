## Tier 3: Underrated Indian Botanicals (Deep Research Findings)

### **Cissus quadrangularis (Hadjod) — The Natural GLP-1 Mimic**
- **Mechanism:** ↑ GLP-1 (+42%), ↓ DPP-4 (−18%), ↑ serotonin → appetite suppression + adipose browning (UCP1 ↑)
- **Dosage:** 300mg standardized extract daily
- **Evidence:** 16-week RCT — −5.8% body weight, −10.9% body fat, comparable to low-dose semaglutide
- **Availability:** CQR-300 extract, Indian brands

### **Sphaeranthus indicus + Garcinia mangostana (Meratrim)**
- **Mechanism:** Dual inhibition of adipogenesis + accelerated lipolysis, ↑ adiponectin (insulin sensitivity)
- **Dosage:** 400mg twice daily with meals
- **Evidence:** Pooled RCTs (n=100) — −5.2kg, −11.9cm waist in 8 weeks

### **Coleus forskohlii + Piperine**
- **Mechanism:** Direct adenylate cyclase activation → cAMP ↑ → HSL phosphorylation → lipolysis; piperine blocks PPARγ (prevents new fat cells), repairs gut barrier, ↑ resting muscle thermogenesis
- **Dosage:** 250mg 10% forskolin extract 2x daily + piperine
- **Evidence:** 12-week RCT in overweight men — ↓ body fat %, ↑ lean mass, ↑ free testosterone
- **⚠️ Warning:** Use standardized forskolin only; raw extract has hepatotoxic compounds

### **Berberis aristata (Daruharidra) / Berberine**
- **Mechanism:** AMPK activation, gut microbiome shift (Firmicutes ↓, Bacteroidetes ↑)
- **Dosage:** 500mg 3x daily before meals + piperine (bioavailability <1% without)
- **Evidence:** 36% metabolic syndrome remission in 3 months
- **⚠️ Major Drug Interaction:** Potent CYP3A4/2D6/2C9 inhibitor — **never combine with statins, immunosuppressants, calcium channel blockers**

### **Withania somnifera (Ashwagandha) — Stress-Adiposity Link**
- **Mechanism:** Cortisol ↓ → visceral fat ↓, cravings ↓; ↑ VO₂ max, ↑ free testosterone (+15.7%), muscle recovery
- **Dosage:** 300mg 2x daily standardized (≥5% withanolides)
- **Evidence:** 24-week RCT — −8.46kg, −3.31 BMI in stressed overweight adults
- **⚠️ Thyroid caution:** Can stimulate thyroid — monitor TSH if prolonged use

## Tier 5: Zero-Cost Environmental Protocols

### **NEAT (Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis)**
- **Impact:** Up to 2,000 kcal/day variance between individuals
- **Protocol:** Stand vs sit (+350 kcal/day); 10–15 min brisk walk **immediately after every major meal** → insulin-independent GLUT4 glucose disposal, blunts postprandial spike, minimizes lipogenesis

### **Intermittent Fasting (16:8 Early Window)**
- **Best:** Eat Window** (e.g., 10 AM – 6 PM)

### **Cold Exposure / Shivering Protocol**
