## For future Claude

Mark Manson's 2016 book, originally a blog post ("Life Lessons from a 40-Year-Old Man Who Just Got Divorced"). Became a global bestseller. Argument structure:
1. We suffer because we pursue the wrong things (entitlements, validation, certainty)
2. Choose better values — improvement, contribution, love, freedom
3. Accept that life is hard, then deal with it

## Core Concepts

- **The Backward Law** — trying to be happy makes you unhappy; trying to be useful makes you useful
- **The Entitlement Fallacy** — believing you deserve better than you get is the root of suffering
- **Choose your suffering** — every value has its cost; pick the suffering you accept
- **Responsibility, not blame** — taking ownership is freedom

## Why It Fits Your Shelf

- Adjacent to [[Meditations]] (Stoicism) and [[Man's Search for Meaning]]
- For your job search / personal crisis periods, the "life is hard" framing is genuinely useful
- Antidote to toxic positivity in self-help
