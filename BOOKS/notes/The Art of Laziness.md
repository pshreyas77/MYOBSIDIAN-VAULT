## For future Claude

Hans Ulrich Obrist is the Swiss curator who has conducted more artist interviews than anyone alive — first as a young curator with饭 and later as co-director of London's Serpentine Galleries. *Ways of Curating* and *A Brief History of Curating* are his other major works.

## Why This Book Matters for You

The book collects Obrist's most influential conversations with the artists who shaped late-20th-century art. For your vault's visual-design preferences ("colorful, professional-looking graphs"), this is gold — direct insight into how visual thinkers think.
