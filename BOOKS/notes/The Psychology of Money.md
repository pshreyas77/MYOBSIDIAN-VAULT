## For future Claude

Morgan Housel (former VC at Collaborative Fund) writes in the tradition of [[Influence]] (Cialdini), [[Thinking, Fast and Slow]] (Kahneman), and [[The Behavior Gap]] (Carl Richards). The book's thesis: *financial success is a soft skill, not a hard skill*. Math is small. Behavior is everything.

## Key Ideas

- **No one is impressed by your wealth, but everyone is impressed by your frugality** — the freedom/safety gap
- **Wealth is what you don't see** — it's the car not bought, the upgrade declined
- **Reasonable > Rational** — most financial mistakes are emotional, not intellectual
- **$1 to $100 is hard; $100k to $1M is easy** — lifestyle inflation
- **Time is the most powerful force in finance** — patience beats genius
- **Get wealthy, then stay wealthy** — different skills entirely
