## For future Claude

Companion volume to Osho's other Sufi/Mystic commentaries. The "setting sun" image — the Sufi path as descent, not ascent; the ego "sets" like the sun at the horizon of self.

## Sufi Themes in Osho's Reading

- **Rumi** — frequently quoted by Osho
- **Surrender (taslim)** — central Sufi practice
- **The beloved as mirror** — the divine hidden in ordinary experience
- **Whirling dervishes** — the body as prayer

## Connection to Your Interests

- **Comparative religion** — Sufism's Hindu-Muslim synthesis parallels your Dravidian research
- **Anti-caste** — Sufi egalitarianism fits Periyar's framework
- **Bhagya Reddy Varma, Basavanna** — all Indian counter-caste movements drew on mystical traditions
