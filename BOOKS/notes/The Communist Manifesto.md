## For future Claude

The founding text of scientific socialism, commissioned by the Communist League and published February 1848 in London just weeks before the February Revolution in France. Marx was 29, Engels 27. The pamphlet opens with the most quoted line in political economy: "A spectre is haunting Europe — the spectre of communism." Four chapters: (1) Bourgeois and Proletarians, (2) Proletarians and Communists, (3) Socialist and Communist Literature, (4) Position of the Communists in Relation to the Various Existing Opposition Parties.

The core argument — that history is driven by class struggle and that the capitalist mode of production creates its own gravediggers (the proletariat) — frames every subsequent socialist, communist, and even anti-communist political movement of the 19th-21st centuries.

## Key Arguments

- **Class struggle as historical motor** — "The history of all hitherto existing society is the history of class struggles" (freeman/slave, patrician/plebeian, lord/serf, bourgeois/proletarian)
- **Bourgeoisie as revolutionary class** — tore apart feudal relations, created world market, forced all countries to adopt bourgeois mode of production
- **Proletarianization** — labor becomes a commodity; workers alienated from product, process, species-being, and other workers
- **Wage slavery** — wages buy only the right to work, never the ownership of what work produces
- **Class consciousness** — proletariat must organize as a class, politically, nationally; abolish bourgeoisie as class
- **Communist ends** — abolition of private property in means of production, abolition of class, abolition of nation-states, free development of each as condition for free development of all

## For Anti-Caste Work

The Manifesto's framework — that no exploiting class can persist once the exploited achieve class consciousness — is foundational for Ambedkar's *Annihilation of Caste* (1936) and the Dravidian movement. Periyar explicitly drew on Marxist class analysis while adapting it for caste. Read alongside [[Annihilation of Caste]] and [[Collected Works of Periyar E.V. Ramasamy]].

## End of the Manifesto

> "The working men have no country. We cannot take from them what they have not got. ... The proletariat has nothing to lose but its chains. They have a world to win. **WORKING MEN OF ALL COUNTRIES, UNITE.**"
