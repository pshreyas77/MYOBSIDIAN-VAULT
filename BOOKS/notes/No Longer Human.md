## For future Claude

Osho's commentary on the distinction between solitude and loneliness. The book argues that modernity has eroded our capacity for genuine aloneness — we are never alone because we are perpetually anxious, entertained, or distracted.

## Core Ideas

- **Aloneness is fullness, loneliness is absence** — they are not opposites
- **Crowd-thinking** — the failure to think independently
- **The ego's investment in suffering** — identity requires problems
- **"No longer"** — the moment a category collapses (single, employed, Indian, etc.)

## Where It Connects

- **Periyar's Self-Respect Movement** — the rejection of imposed identity parallels "no longer"
- **Bhagat Singh's atheism** — rejection of religious identity as foundational
- **Charvaka/Lokayata** — the rejection of spiritual authority parallels Osho's "no teacher"
