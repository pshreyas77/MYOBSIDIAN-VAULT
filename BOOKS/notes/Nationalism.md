## For future Claude

James Fallows (longtime Atlantic journalist) writes about American political culture as distinct from European: Americans have a "nation" but not an "ideology"; they have constitutional patriotism, not dogma. Falls argues this is *the* American advantage — flexibility, pluralism, individualism without the religious/civil wars that broke Europe.

## Why It Fits Your Interests

- **Nationalism vs ideology** — directly relevant to your Dravidian political identity research
- **Constitutional patriotism (Verfassungspatriotismus)** — German concept Dolf Sternberger coined 1979, parallel to Fallows
- **Indian politics** — Hindutva is exactly the kind of "ideology" Fallows argues America avoided
