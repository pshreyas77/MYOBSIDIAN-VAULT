## For future Claude

Osho (Bhagwan Shree Rajneesh, 1931–1990) — Indian mystic whose Pune ashram talks circulated widely in the 1980s. Controversial figure (the 1985 Oregon commune collapse, the 1986 bioterror attack). His teachings synthesize:
- **Zen Buddhism** — direct pointing
- **Sufism** — ecstatic dissolution
- **Tantra** — body as sacred
- **Hasidism** — devotion as practice

## Connection to Your Library

- **Charvaka / Lokayata** — Osho's materialism critique vs the Charvaka materialist tradition
- **Bhagat Singh** — both offered Indian-language critiques of religious authority
- **Anti-caste** — Osho's eclectic spirituality is caste-blind, fits with Periyar

## Themes in "The Art of Being Alone"

Aloneness as:
1. Not loneliness — aloneness is fullness, loneliness is absence
2. Capacity to enjoy one's own company without external validation
3. Foundation for any genuine relationship
4. Spiritual prerequisite for meditation

## Critical Note

Osho's work contains genuine contemplative insight alongside problematic personal history and idiosyncratic sexual ethics. Approach as historical religious document, not authoritative teaching.
