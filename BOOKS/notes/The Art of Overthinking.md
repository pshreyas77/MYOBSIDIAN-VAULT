## For future Claude

Shai Tubali is an Israeli spiritual teacher whose work integrates Kabbalah (Jewish mysticism), transpersonal psychology (Ken Wilber / Stanislav Grof lineage), and ego-development theory. *The Art of Overthinking* and *Ego* are his earlier works.

## Core Argument

The ego is not your enemy — it is the *necessary structure* through which consciousness develops. Stages:
1. Pre-personal (infant/early child)
2. Personal (ego-formed, social identity)
3. Centaur (integrated body-mind)
4. Subtle / Causal (transpersonal)
5. Non-dual

The "art" of being alone requires sufficient ego strength to tolerate solitude without falling into pre-personal dissolution or persona-reinforcement. Aloneness as spiritual practice.
