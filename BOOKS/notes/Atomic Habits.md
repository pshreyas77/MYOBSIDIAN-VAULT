## For future Claude

James Clear's 2018 book built from his popular newsletter and years of habit-tracking research. Complementary to (and in places a refinement of) [[The Power of Habit]] by Charles Duhigg. More actionable; less corporate-anecdote.

## The Four Laws (Foundation)

Every habit follows a four-step loop:
1. **Cue** — triggers the behavior
2. **Craving** — motivational force
3. **Response** — the behavior itself
4. **Reward** — satisfies the craving

Build good habits by making cues obvious, cravings attractive, responses easy, rewards satisfying. Break bad habits by inversion.

## Key Concepts

- **Identity-based habits** — "I am a reader" beats "I want to read more"
- **1% improvements** — compound interest applied to daily actions
- **Habit stacking** — attach new habit to existing one
- **Environment design** — make good behaviors obvious, bad ones invisible
- **Plateau of latent potential** — biggest gains appear after breakthrough moments, not linear progression
