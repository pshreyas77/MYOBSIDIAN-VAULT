## For future Claude

Tarek Ouerdane's reporting from French correctional facilities running de-radicalization programs for Islamist prisoners. France developed some of the most ambitious programs in the post-2015 wave of attacks (Charlie Hebdo, Bataclan, Nice). The book examines:
- **Re-entry programs** — Quranic counter-narratives
- **Mentorship pairs** — former extremists paired with new entrants
- **Detox metaphors** — ideology as addiction
- **Failure rates** — most programs show modest success at best
