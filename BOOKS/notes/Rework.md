## For future Claude

Cal Newport (computer science professor at Georgetown, author of *Digital Minimalism* and *So Good They Can't Ignore You*) writes about deep work — sustained, focused, cognitively demanding work that produces rare and valuable output. The book argues:
1. Deep work is increasingly rare in the modern economy
2. Deep work is increasingly valuable in the information economy
3. Therefore, the ability to do deep work is now a superpower

## Core Principles

- **Deep work** — cognitively demanding, distraction-free, professional-grade output
- **Shallow work** — non-cognitively demanding, logistical, often performed while distracted
- **The four philosophies**: monastic, bimodal, rhythmic, journalistic
- **Quality of life** — deep work produces the kind of work that gives meaning

## Why It Fits You

- **Hermes automation** — your system IS the embodiment of deep-work principles (let machines handle shallow work)
- **Obsidian vault** — atomic notes require deep work sessions
- **AI/ML portfolio** — your job search benefits from deep, sustained focus
- **VerifAI, OmniResearch** — flagship projects need sustained deep work
