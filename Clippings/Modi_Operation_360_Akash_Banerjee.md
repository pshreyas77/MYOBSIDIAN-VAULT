# (2) Modi's Operation 360 — How Close Is BJP To Gaining Absolute Power In Parliament? | Akash Banerjee

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Akash Banerjee's investigation into **"Operation 360"** — the BJP's alleged coordinated strategy to reach **360 Lok Sabha seats** (two-thirds majority) to amend the Constitution at will. The video connects:
- Women's Reservation Bill (106th Amendment) as a Trojan horse
- Delimitation (850 seats) as the real objective
- Anti-defection law loopholes (2/3rds merger clause)
- Systematic horse-trading of opposition MPs
- Constitutional amendments that could end federalism

---

## 🔑 Key Arguments

### 1. The 360 Target
- **Two-thirds majority (360/543)** needed for Constitutional amendments under Article 368
- Current NDA: ~293 Lok Sabha, 106 Rajya Sabha
- Target: 360 Lok Sabha + 164 Rajya Sabha

### 2. The Trojan Horse: Women's Reservation + Delimitation
| Bill | Stated Goal | Hidden Agenda |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| 106th Amendment (2023) | Women's 33% reservation | Delays implementation until Census + Delimitation |
| 131st Amendment (2026) | Increase seats 543→850 + women's quota | **Delimitation based on population = Hindi belt dominance** |

**Key quote:** *"Under the guise of women's empowerment and reservation, the game of delimitation is being orchestrated."*

### 3. The Numbers Game — Hindi Belt vs South India

| Region | Current Seats | Post-Delimitation (850) | Change |
|--------|---------------|-------------------------|--------|
| **Hindi Belt** | 207 | **311** | **+104** |
| **South India** | 132 | **198** | +66 |
| **UP + Bihar alone** | — | **>200** | — |

**Hindi belt gains 113-seat lead over South India.** South India never crosses 200 seats.

### 4. The Anti-Defection Law Loophole (Operation 360)
| Step | Mechanism |
|------|-----------|
| 1 | 2/3rds of a party's MPs form separate group (para 4 of 10th Schedule) |
| 2 | New faction merges with small registered party (e.g., Nationalist Citizen Party) |
| 3 | Faction merges into NDA — **anti-defection law doesn't apply** |
| 4 | MPs don't formally join BJP → **plausible deniability** |

**Examples cited:**
- **TMC:** 20/28 MPs → rebel faction led by Kakoli Ghosh Dastidar → merge with Nationalist Citizen Party → NDA
- **Shiv Sena (UBT):** 6/9 MPs → merge with Shinde Sena → NDA
- **AAP (Punjab):** 7/10 Rajya Sabha MPs → BJP
- **Bengal:** 3 Rajya Sabha MPs resign, win on BJP ticket

### 5. Constitutional Consequences of 360
If 360 reached → three dangerous bills:
1. **One Nation, One Election** — wipe out regional parties
2. **130th Amendment** — auto-disqualification on mere allegation + 30 days jail (ED/CBI weaponization)
3. **Rajya Sabha neutering** — joint sitting dominance of Lok Sabha halves Rajya Sabha power

### 6. The Constitutional Betrayal
- **1976 (Indira Gandhi) & 2001 (Vajpayee)** froze seats at 1971 Census (543) to protect South India
- **South India controlled population** — penalized for success
- **North India population exploded** — rewarded with 104 extra seats
