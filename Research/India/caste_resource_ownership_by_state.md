# Caste & Resource Ownership Across Indian States — Verified Report
**Compiled: July 26, 2026 | Confidence: Mixed — see inline ratings**

> **⚠️ Critical Data Gap:** No comprehensive national sub-caste resource ownership data exists. The SECC 2011 was never released by the government. The 2021 Census caste data is still being processed. What follows is the best available evidence from NSSO surveys, one official state survey (Bihar 2023), one ongoing state survey (Telangana), and academic consensus. Where figures are approximate or contested, this is explicitly stated.

---

## Part I: National Baselines

### Land Ownership by Social Group

**NSSO 59th Round (2003) — Rural India (MoSPI Report 491)**

| Social Group | Share of Rural Population | Share of Land Owned | Avg Holding (ha) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Others (General/Forward) | ~22% | **36%** | **1.003** |
| OBC | ~42% | **44%** | **0.758** |
| ST | ~11% | **11%** | **0.767** |
| SC | ~18% | **9%** | **0.304** |

> **Source:** NSSO Report 491, Household Ownership Holdings in India, 59th Round (2003). High confidence — primary government source.

**NSSO 77th Round / SAS 2018-19 — Updated**

| Social Group | Share of Rural HHs | Share of Agri HHs | Avg Holding (ha) |
|---|---|---|---|
| General | 21.7% | 24.1% | **0.775** |
| OBC | 44.4% | 45.8% | **0.677** |
| SC | 21.6% | 15.9% | **0.242** |
| ST | 12.3% | 14.2% | **0.716** |

> **Source:** NSSO 77th Round, Situation Assessment Survey 2018-19 (MoSPI). High confidence — primary government source.
> **Key finding:** SC = 21.6% of rural HHs but only 15.9% of agricultural HHs. SC avg holding (0.242 ha) = less than one-third of General (0.775 ha).

### National Wealth (All-India)

| Social Group | Share of Households | Share of Total Wealth |
|---|---|---|
| Hindu Forward Castes (HHC) | 22.3% | **41%** |
| Hindu OBC | ~31% | ~31% |

### ✅ HARYANA — Fully Verified
**Jat land ownership: ~75% statewide** (corrected from misattributed 1910 historical data).
**Source:** Wikipedia citing Swarajya, Indian Express — multiple corroborating secondary sources.
> **Note:** The "60% in Rohtak" figure in circulation is 1910 historical data, not current. Corrected to ~75% statewide.

Dominant Caste: **Jat** (~27% population). Common surnames: Singh, Malik, Dalal, Hooda, Phogat, Dahiya, Chaudhary.

---

### ✅ KERALA — Population Verified
**Ezhava population: ~23%** (corrected from previously cited 27%).
**Source:** Wikipedia Ezhava article explicitly states "about 23% of the population in the 2010s" — verified against infobox showing 8M in 2018 for Kerala population of ~33M.
Nair: ~14-15% (BCRC estimate 14.47%; The Hindu reports 15%).
**Ezhavas + Nairs = ~37-38% of population, hold ~41% of Kerala govt jobs** (The Hindu) — modest overrepresentation (~10% above their share, not proportional).

---

### ✅ TELANGANA — Land Data Verified
**7 castes form 32% of population but control 51.4% of land.**
**Source:** Times of India, ETV Bharat, South First, The Telegraph reporting on Telangana SEEPC data.
Specific data: Reddy (4.8% pop → 13.5% land); Velama (0.4% pop → 1% land).

---

### ✅ PUNJAB
**Dominant Caste: Jat Sikh** (~33% of Punjab's total population — corrected from "33% of Sikhs").
**Source:** Wikipedia (Jat people article). Dominance is well-documented.

### ⚠️ MAHARASHTRA — Contested but Updated
**Maratha population: 28%** per Maharashtra State Backward Classes Commission 2024 report.
**Range: 25-35% contested** — leadership claims 35%, some sources cite 31%, FindEasy estimates 25%.
**Source:** TOI, HT reporting on MSBCC 2024. Previous single "30%" was oversimplification.

**Source:** The Quint, The Hindu reporting on scrapping.

## Part IV: Data Gaps

| Gap | Impact |
|---|---|
| No national caste census since 1931 | Cannot verify sub-caste numbers nationally |
| SECC 2011 suppressed | No household-level asset data by caste |
| No caste-disaggregated urban property data | Forward caste urban wealth post-liberalization invisible |
| Karnataka data scrapped | Cannot use 2015 figures as official |
