## 📋 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Bhagya Reddy Varma (1888–1939)** was the pioneering Dalit leader of Hyderabad State, founder of the **Adi-Hindu movement**, and the first to systematically organize the Mala/Madiga communities against untouchability in the Nizam's dominion.

**Critical Finding on Written Works:** Despite extensive activism (3,348 speeches claimed, 25 schools, multiple conferences), **no self-authored English books, pamphlets, or articles are discoverable in accessible digital archives.** His work was primarily:
- **Oral** (Harikatha storytelling, speeches in Telugu/Urdu)
- **Organizational** (institution-building, petitions to Nizam)
- **Documented by others** (British records, Nizam's farmans, later academic works)

## 🗣️ NOTABLE QUOTES (Attributed in Secondary Sources)

> *"If the Untouchables make no noise, the Hindu feels no shame for their condition... But if the Untouchables rise and ask for recognition, he is prepared to deny their existence, repudiate his responsibility and refuse to share his power without feeling any compunction or remorse."*
> — **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar** (cited as the epigraph framing Varma's work in Ghanta 2013)

> *"We are not Mala, not Madiga, not Untouchable. We are **Adi Hindus** — the Original Hindus of this land."*
> — **Bhagya Reddy Varma** (1912 Adi-Hindu Manifesto, paraphrased in Kshīrasāgara 1994)
