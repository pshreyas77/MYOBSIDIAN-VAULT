# Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) — Monsoon Session "Chalo Sansad" March

> **Watch out:** The acronym **CJP** also belongs to Teesta Setalvad's *Citizens for Justice and Peace*. This note is about the **Cockroach Janta Party** — a youth-led satirical protest movement, formed May 16, 2026.

---

## TL;DR

On July 20, 2026, the youth-led Cockroach Janta Party attempted a "Chalo Sansad" (March to Parliament) on Day 1 of the Monsoon Session. 10,000-20,000 marched from Jantar Mantar. Delhi Police used tear gas and lathicharge; mass detentions occurred. **Internet shutdown around Jantar Mantar** reported. Talks held with BJP's JP Nadda but who initiated them is disputed. Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan's resignation remains the central demand, linked to NEET-UG paper-leak scandal.

## 2. Origin (May 15–16, 2026)

**May 15, 2026:** CJI Justice Surya Kant, hearing a contempt plea, made remarks about people entering "legal, media, social media and other noble professions" using fake degrees, comparing them to **"cockroaches" and "parasites of society."**

**Direct CJI quote (Wikipedia):**
> "There are youngsters like cockroaches; they don't get any employment, and they don't have any place in a profession. Some of them become media, some of them become social media, some of them become RTI activists, some of them become other activists..."

**May 16, 2026:** Abhijeet Dipke launched CJP as:
- Satirical parody of the insult ("voice of the Lazy & Unemployed")
- Wordplay on ruling party name (Bharatiya **Janata** Party → Cockroach **Janata** Party)

**Clarification:** CJI Surya Kant subsequently clarified his remarks were specifically about **fake-degree holders**, not all youth, and praised Indian youth.

## 3. The Movement's Anatomy

### What CJP Is
- **NOT** a registered political party (per Election Commission of India)
- **Youth cultural-satirical movement** with political demands
- Tactical platform: satire → viral memes → on-ground mobilisation

### What It Demands (5-point manifesto)
1. Resignation of Dharmendra Pradhan (Education Minister)
2. Resignation of Narendra Modi
3. Parliamentary debate on student issues
4. Right to employment law (21-60 yr olds)
5. Transparency under RTI in education/exams (Bhardwaj addition)

### Eligibility Criteria (satirical)
Unemployed + lazy + chronically online + can rant professionally.

### Ideological Position
"Secular, Socialist, Democratic, and Lazy."

## 5. The March: July 20, 2026

### What Happened (Verified)
- 10,000-20,000 protesters at Jantar Mantar, marching toward Parliament
- Police used **tear gas + lathicharge**
- **Mass detentions** (Al Jazeera, Scroll videos)
- **Mobile internet shutdown** ordered around Jantar Mantar
- Metro exits near Jantar Mantar reportedly **sealed**; protesters got off "a few stations away"

### Disputed / Unverified
- **Reuters** reports stone-pelting by protesters — sequence vs. police action not fully clear from sources
- Specific **injury count** not confirmed by Hematologic relief organisations (none cited by name)
- **Internet shutdown timing** — pre-emptive vs. reactive; sources conflict
- **CJP claim: "marched with restraint"** vs Delhi Police claim: "protesters were unruly, aggressive, and violent" — both sides contest

### Talks
- BJP's **JP Nadda** met CJP leaders
- **Disputed who initiated** — Nadda says government; CJP says youth delegates
- Talks via crossover with **Health Minister** JP Nadda (not Education Minister Pradhan)

### Outcome
- Modal assurance from government **NOT** full Pradhan resignation
- **Wangchuk's fast continues**
- **Dipke ended his fast** (~14 hours)
- **Solidarity protests** confirmed in Mumbai + Shillong

## 6. Constitutional Question (Plausible Framework, NOT Court Verdicts)

### Settled Law (Real, Cited Correctly)
- **Article 19(1)(b):** Freedom to assemble peaceably
- **Article 19(1)(c):** Freedom to move freely
- **Anuradha Bhasin v. UoI (2020):** Internet shutdowns must satisfy **proportionality test**
- **KS Puttaswamy v. UoI (2017):** Right to privacy as fundamental right
- **Article 22:** Preventive detention requires specific individual grounds
