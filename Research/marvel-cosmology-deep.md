## 2. All Abstract Entities

### The Four Pillars of Reality (Seventh Cosmos Compass)

#### **Eternity** — Embodiment of Time & the Universe
- **Multiversal Eternity**: Represents the entire Multiverse in current iteration (7th/8th Cosmos)
- Each reality has its own Eternity; all are parts of the greater multiversal Eternity
- Sister: Infinity; Opposes: Death
- Children: Epoch, Atman, Dragus, Abraxas (malevolent), Now, Then, etc.
- **Key Feats**: Contains all timelines of a universe; fought Thanos w/ Infinity Gauntlet; judged by Living Tribunal
- **Official Handbook**: "Eternity is the sum total of all life and matter in the universe, the personification of time itself" (OHOTMU A-Z #4)

#### **Infinity** — Embodiment of Space & the Universe
- **Multiversal Infinity**: Represents space across the Multiverse
- Brother: Eternity; Opposes: Oblivion
- Each reality has its own Infinity; all part of greater multiversal Infinity
- **Key Feats**: Equal to Eternity; together represent space-time continuum
- **Official Handbook**: "Infinity is the personification of space, complement to Eternity's time" (OHOTMU A-Z #5)

#### **Death** — Personification of Death
- **Multiversal Death**: Single entity across Multiverse (unlike Eternity/Infinity)
- Opposes: Eternity; Sister: Life
- Avatar: Marlo Chandler, Phil Coulson (recent)
- **Key Feats**: Thanos sought to impress her with Infinity Gauntlet snap; cannot be truly killed; claims all souls
- **Official Handbook**: "Death is the abstract embodiment of the cessation of life" (OHOTMU)

#### **Oblivion** — Personification of Nothingness/Void
- Predates creation; "Before there was everything, there was Oblivion"
- Opposes: Infinity; Embodies the Oblivion Void/Far Shore
- Intermediary: The Preordained
- **Key Feats**: Witnessed multiple renewal cycles; Chaos King, Knull, Nyx are merely beings *of* the void, Oblivion *is* the void
- **Official Handbook**: "Oblivion is the personification of non-existence, the void before creation"

### Higher Abstracts

#### **Living Tribunal**
- **Role**: Multiversal Arbiter, Representative of One-Above-All
- **Structure**: Three faces (Equity, Vengeance, Necessity) — must agree to act
- **Authority**: Highest within Multiverse; judges cosmic-scale threats
- **Feats**: 
  - Disassembled Infinity Gauntlet (declared gems too dangerous together)
  - Annihilated by Beyonders (along with all abstracts across Multiverse)
  - Restored via Adam Warlock (Earth-19141) deal with Above-All-Others
- **Quote**: "There is only one Living Tribunal in the Multiverse, as it exists in all dimensions and realities simultaneously"

#### **First Firmament**
- **Origin**: Personification of the **First Cosmos** (first universe ever)
- **Nature**: Stagnant, eternal, opposed change/mortality
- **Conflict**: Celestial War — Celestials wanted evolution, Firmament wanted stasis
- **Result**: Celestials shattered Firmament → created **Second Cosmos** (First Multiverse)
- **Remnants**: Chains binding Mother of Horrors in Requiem Plain made of First Firmament
- **Status**: Survives in fragmented form; ancestor of Queen of Nevers

#### **Queen of Nevers (Never Queen)**
- **Origin**: Former **Pilgrim** — Sentience of **Fourth Cosmos**
- **Role**: Embodiment of **Possibility** ("What Might Be")
- **Domain**: Land of Couldn't-Be-Shouldn't-Be (outside Multiverse)
- **Agents**: Nexus Beings (Franklin Richards, Scarlet Witch, etc.)
- **Counterpart**: Griever at the End of All Things (sister)
- **Feats**: Carried Archetypes of Fourth Cosmos into Mystery; heart stolen by Incredulous Zed; restored by Dawn Greenwood

#### **Griever at the End of All Things**
- **Role**: Embodiment of **Entropy, Heat Death, End of All Things**
- **Philosophy**: "The End is the Truth" / "Quiet Math"
- **Agents**: Endlings
- **Counterpart**: Queen of Nevers (sister)
- **Feats**: Destroyed hundreds of realities created by Future Foundation; killed Molecule Man; follows Franklin Richards

#### **Phoenix Force**
- **Nature**: Fire and Life; Creation AND Destruction
- **Role**: Cosmic force of rebirth; bonds with hosts (Jean Grey, etc.)
- **Domain**: White Hot Room (outside Multiverse, in Mystery)
- **Feats**: Destroyed/recreated universes; Dark Phoenix saga; AvX; necessary for multiversal renewal

## 3. Multiverse Structure: 7th Cosmos, Branching Timelines, Omniverse Definition

### The Marvel Multiverse (Seventh/Eighth Cosmos)

**Structure within a Single Cosmos:**
```
MULTIVERSE
├── Superflow (Inter-universal medium; "white space"; where Mapmakers travel)
├── Neutral Zone (Boundary of Multiverse; matter/antimatter coexist)
├── Overspace (Realm of abstract manifestations; above Multiverse)
├── Dimension of Manifestations (M-Bodies created by Anthropomorpho)
├── Primary Realities (Earth-616, Earth-1610, Earth-838, etc.)
│   ├── Alternate Timelines (Divergent histories)
│   ├── Pocket Dimensions (Microverse, Negative Zone, Dark Dimension, etc.)
│   └── Sub-Realities (Pocket universes within universes)
��── Incursion Mechanics: Earths serve as focal points for universe collisions
```

**Key Definitions (Official Handbook):**
- **Universe/Continuity**: Single reality (e.g., Earth-616). Not same as "dimension" or "galaxy" — Mephisto/Dormammu/Celestials all within Earth-616.
- **Multiverse**: Collection of alternate universes with similar nature and universal hierarchy. Contains Earth-616, most What If? universes, vast alternate Earths.
- **Megaverse**: Structure between Multiverse and Omniverse linking closely-associated multiverses (e.g., Shadowline) and more distant ones. Fluid definition.
- **Omniverse**: **Collection of EVERYTHING** — every universe, multiverse, megaverse, dimension, realm, including non-Marvel properties (DC, Image, Dragon Ball, Naruto, real world, fan fiction, unpublished works, thoughts). **Only ONE Omniverse exists.**

> **Omniverse Quote** (Official Handbook of the Marvel Universe: Alternate Universes 2005): *"It includes every single literary work, television show, movie, urban legend, universe, realm, etc. ever. It includes everyone from Popeye to Rocky Balboa to Ronald Reagan to Romeo and Juliet to Luke Skywalker to Snoopy to Jay and Silent Bob, etc."*

**Omniverse Clusters**: The Omniverse divides into multiverse clusters governed by their creators:
- Marvel Multiverse → One Above All
- Jujutsu Kaisen setting → Magic/Death cluster
- Cthulhu Mythos → "Chaos Womb" cluster presided by Blind Idiot God (Azathoth)
- Etc.

### Branching Timelines
- **Mechanism**: Every decision/event spawns alternate timelines (What If? realities)
- **Classification**: Earth-616 = Prime Marvel Universe; others numbered (Earth-1610 Ultimate, Earth-838 MCU 838, etc.)
- **Incursion Focal Point**: During Time Runs Out, **Earths** became collision points for universe incursions
- **Nexus Beings**: Entities whose choices affect probability across timelines (Scarlet Witch, Franklin Richards, Kang, etc.) — agents of Queen of Nevers

### The 7th Cosmos (Seventh Multiverse) → 8th Cosmos Transition

**Seventh Cosmos:**
- Embodied by **dual Eternity & Infinity** (not single entity)
- Destroyed by **Beyonders' "Perfect Annihilation Plan"**
- Incursions caused by Molecule Men deaths (Beyonder-created multiversal bomb)
- Only two realities remained: Earth-616 and Earth-1610

**Eighth Cosmos (Current):**
- Restored by **Reed Richards + Franklin Richards + Molecule Man** (using Beyonders' power)
- **Continuation** of Seventh (unusual — normally new cosmos starts from nothing)
- Some cosmic beings don't believe it's truly "Eighth" (minor differences only)
- **New Hierarchy**: Four Ur-Divisor pairs (see Section 2)
- Embodied by **same Eternity/Infinity** (unprecedented — normally new embodiment)

## 4. Cosmic Hierarchy: Celestials, Watchers, Abstracts, Tribunal, OAA

### Official Hierarchy (Per Cosmic Entity Page & Kubik)

```
TIER 0: ONE ABOVE ALL (Above-All-Others)
    └── Creator of Multiverse; House of Ideas; only weapon is love
    └── Dark Aspect: One Below All (hate)

TIER 1: LIVING TRIBUNAL
    └── Servant/Representative of OAA
    └── Multiversal Arbiter; three faces must agree
    └── Leads court of cosmic beings

TIER 2: ETERNITY & INFINITY (Multiversal)
    └── Dual embodiments of Space-Time of current Cosmos
    └── Each universe has its own aspect; all part of greater whole

TIER 3: MASTER ORDER & LORD CHAOS
    └── Personifications of Order/Chaos
    └── Created In-Betweener as mediator

TIER 4: GALACTUS, CELESTIALS, WATCHERS, STRANGER, IN-BETWEENER
    └── Grouped together per Kubik/Quasar #37
    └── Abstracts NOT automatically > Non-abstracts (Eon noted as weak)
    └── Galactus: Survivor of 6th Cosmos; Power Cosmic; Devourer of Worlds
    └── Celestials: Created by First Firmament; 2000 ft tall armor; shaped life across all Cosmos
    └── Watchers: Non-interference oath; vast cosmic power
    └── Stranger: Gestalt of Gigantian Eternals; Laboratory World
    └── In-Betweener: Balance mediator; created by Order/Chaos

TIER 5: LESSER ABSTRACTS / COSMIC CUBES / SHAPER OF WORLDS
    └── Cosmic Cubes: Reality-warping artifacts; can become sentient (Shaper)
    └── Kubik: "Cosmic Cubes rank below Celestials" (but can affect Celestials/Galactus/In-Betweener)

TIER 6: ELDERS OF THE UNIVERSE
    └── Survivors of oldest races; Power Primordial; immortal
    └── Grandmaster, Collector, Champion, Gardener, etc.
    └── Rank at bottom of cosmic table (Grandmaster noted possibility of losing seat)

TIER 7: MORTALS WITH COSMIC ARTIFACTS (Infinity Gauntlet, Heart of the Universe, etc.)
    └── Thanos w/ IG: Usurped almost all cosmic beings including Eternity
    └   └── Black Vortex: Can elevate mortal to cosmic entity
```

### Celestials — Deep Dive

- **Origin**: First beings created by **First Firmament** (First Cosmos)
- **Schism**: **Aspirants** (loyal to Firmament, wanted stasis) vs **Celestials** (wanted change/mortality/evolution)
- **Celestial War**: Shattered First Firmament → created **Second Cosmos** (First Multiverse)
- **Role**: "Space gods" — 2000 ft tall armored beings; cosmic genetic engineers
- **Function**: Seed life, guide evolution, judge worlds (Celestial Hosts)
- **Pass Through ALL Cosmoses**: Survive multiversal renewals; involved in creation of each new iteration
- **Created Servitors**: **Omegas** (became **Beyonders/Ivory Kings**) to maintain Second Cosmos
- **Notable**: Arishem (Judge), Exitar (Exterminator), Tiamut (Dreaming Celestial), One Above All (Celestial leader — **NOT** the supreme being)

### Watchers

- **Oath**: "Only watch, never interfere" (broken by Uatu repeatedly)
- **Power**: Vast cosmic awareness, energy manipulation, virtual immortality
- **Base**: Moon (Uatu), various observation posts across Multiverse
- **Notable**: Uatu (Earth-616 Watcher), Aron (Renegade), The One (Leader)
- **Hierarchy**: Peer to Celestials/Galactus per Kubik

### Abstracts vs. Non-Abstracts

> **Quasar #37 Clarification**: "Galactus, Celestials, the Stranger, Watchers, etc. are **cosmic beings** but **NOT abstract entities** in the strict sense." Abstracts = conceptual personifications (Eternity, Death, etc.). Cosmic beings = entities with cosmic power regardless of origin.

## 5. Beyonders, First Firmament, Ivory Kings

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|

- Original Secret Wars (1984): Single Beyonder (child unit) created Battleworld

### First Firmament

- **Personification of First Cosmos** (first universe)
- **Stagnant, eternal, opposed change**

### Ivory Kings = Beyonders

> **Direct Quote**: *"For we are the Celestial Servitors. We are the Omega Council. We are the Ivory Kings. We are from Beyond."*
- **Omega Council**: Governing body of Beyonders

- **Black Swans**: Disciples of Rabum Alal (Doom); originally served Beyonders' Library of Worlds

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|

## 9. Official Handbook Definitions

### Official Handbook of the Marvel Universe (OHOTMU)

**History**: 1982-present; Mark Gruenwald editor; detailed stats for characters/items/locations; basis for Marvel Database Project.

**Key Definitions from OHOTMU**:

#### **Universe/Continuity**
> *"A Universe/continuity is a single reality, such as Earth-616, the mainstream Marvel Universe/Continuity. In Marvel Comics, the concept of a continuity is not the same as 'dimension' or 'galaxy'; for example, characters like Mephisto and Dormammu hail from alternate dimensions and the Celestials from another galaxy, but they all nevertheless belong to Universe-616."*

#### **Multiverse**
> *"A Multiverse is a collection of alternate universes, with a similar nature and a universal hierarchy. The Marvel multiverse contains Earth-616 and most of the What If? universes, as well as the vast number of alternate Marvel Universe Earths."*

#### **Megaverse**
> *"The Megaverse is a structure present between Multiverse and Omniverse which links realities more closely associated to the mainstream Multiverse, and more distant realities, such as the Shadowline."*

#### **Omniverse**
> *"The Omniverse is the collection of every single universe, multiverse, megaverse, dimension (alternate or pocket), and realm. This includes not only Marvel Comics, but also DC Comics, Image, Dark Horse, Wildstorm, Archie, Harvey, Shueisha, Boom Studios, Rebellion, Dynamite, IDW, Graphic India, Derby Pop, Vertigo, Oni Press, Udon, Valiant, Kodansha, Shogakukan, and every universe ever mentioned or seen (and an infinite amount never mentioned or seen) including our own world. Everything is in the Omniverse, and there is only one Omniverse."*

> *"It includes every single literary work, television show, movie, urban legend, universe, realm, etc. ever. It includes everyone from Popeye to Rocky Balboa to Ronald Reagan to Romeo and Juliet to Luke Skywalker to Snoopy to Jay and Silent Bob, etc."*

#### **Abstract Entities (Quasar #37 Clarification)**
> *"Galactus, Celestials, the Stranger, Watchers, etc. are cosmic beings but NOT abstract entities in the strict sense."*
> *"Abstract entities are conceptual cosmic beings that do not have physical form. They manifest through 'M-Bodies' provided from the Dimension of Manifestations."*

#### **Cosmic Hierarchy (Kubik / Cosmic Entity Entry)**
> *"There is a loose hierarchy among the cosmic beings. While the highest cosmic being is known as the One Above All... it has a servant called the Living Tribunal, who acts as the arbiter for the Multiverse and leads a court of prominent cosmic beings. Ranking below the Tribunal are the dual embodiments of the universe: Eternity and Infinity. After them are other major abstracts, such as Master Order and Lord Chaos. Below them, beings like Galactus, the Celestials, and the Watchers are grouped together, as well as 'lesser abstracts' like the In-Betweener. Abstracts are not automatically more powerful than non-abstracts."*

#### **Cosmic Cubes**
> *"According to Kubik, Cosmic Cubes rank below Celestials, although there are known cases of them working on Celestials, Galactus, and the In-Betweener."*

#### **Multiversal Renewal**
> *"A cosmic renewal can shift the hierarchy, rendering it in flux temporarily, which Galactus took advantage of to defeat Master Order and Lord Chaos, who are normally above him in the hierarchy."*

#### **One Above All (OHOTMU A-Z)**
> *"The One Above All is the highest creative force in known existence and the source of all there is, was, and ever will be. It is said to be more powerful than all its creations combined. Normally, the One Above All's servant, the Living Tribunal, acts on its behalf as the supreme authority of the Multiverse."*
