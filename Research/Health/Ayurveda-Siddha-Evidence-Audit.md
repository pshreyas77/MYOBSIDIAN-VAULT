## 2. Critical Safety Update: Heavy Metal Contamination

**JAMA 2004** and multiple follow-ups found chronic Ayurvedic formulations contaminated with lead, mercury, and arsenic.

| Issue | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Not "traditional"** | This is adulteration from improper manufacturing |
| **Rasashastra exception** | Metallic/mineral preparations intentionally use mercury, lead, arsenic in traditional texts |
| **Bhasmas** | Calcined metals claim detoxification through incineration; **scientific validation is absent** |
| **Regulatory gap** | AYUSH ministry does not require heavy metal testing for all products |

**Recommendation**: Avoid all rasashastra/bhasma preparations. Use only plant-based formulations from standardized manufacturers with third-party testing.

## 3. Siddha Specifics

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Less evidence than Ayurveda** | Very few RCTs; most studies are traditional case reports |
| **Higher toxicity risk** | Siddha uses more metallic preparations than Ayurveda; muppu (triple salt) and parpam (calcined metals) are common |
| **Regional specificity** | Tamil Nadu traditional medicine; overlaps with Ayurvedic herbs but distinct formulations |
