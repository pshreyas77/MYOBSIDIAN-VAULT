# South Asian Genomic Health Protocol

**For future Claude:** Cross-domain from ancestry research (Cross-Domain Idea #7). ANI/ASI/AASI ancestry proportions correlate with metabolic traits. Use as heuristic, not deterministic — genotype is not destiny; environment and behavior dominate.

---

## 1. Ancestry-Informed Adjustments

| Trait | Variant | South Asian Pattern | Clinical Action |
|-------|---------|---------------------|-----------------|
| **Type 2 Diabetes Risk** | TCF7L2, PPARG, KCNQ1 | Higher risk at lower BMI ("Asian Indian phenotype": insulin resistance at BMI 23–25) | Screen at BMI ≥23, not 25; HbA1c at 35+ |
| **Lactase Persistence** | LCT -13910T | ~30–40% in North India; <20% in South India | Most South Asians are lactose intolerant as adults; avoid dairy or use lactase |
| **Beta-Thalassemia Carrier** | HBB gene | Carrier frequency 3–17% (Gujarat, Sindh, Punjab, Bengal) | Premarital/carrier screening essential |
| **Familial Hypercholesterolemia** | APOB, LDLR | Founder mutations in some communities (Gujarati, Punjabi) | Cascade screening; early statin therapy |
| **CYP2C19 Metabolism** | *2, *3 alleles | Poor metabolizers: 13–20% of South Asians | Clopidogrel ineffective; use alternative antiplatelet |
| **Vitamin D Deficiency** | VDR variants + skin pigmentation | Deficiency rates >70% in India | Supplementation: 2000–4000 IU/day; monitor 25(OH)D |
| **G6PD Deficiency** | G6PD variants | 0–20% depending on region; higher in tribal populations | Avoid primaquine, dapsone, certain antibiotics |

## 2. Pharmacogenomics (South Asian Specific)

| Drug | Gene | Variant | Impact |
|------|------|---------|--------|
| **Clopidogrel** | CYP2C19 | *2, *3 | Reduced efficacy; use ticagrelor/prasugrel |
| **Warfarin** | CYP2C9, VKORC1 | Multiple | Higher dose requirements; INR monitoring critical |
| **Codeine** | CYP2D6 | *4, *10 | Poor metabolizers get no pain relief; ultra-rapid at risk of toxicity |
| **Carbamazepine** | HLA-B | *15:02 | Stevens-Johnson syndrome risk in some South Asian groups |

## 3. Honest Limitations

| Limitation | Reality |
|------------|---------|
| **Not clinically validated** | Ancestry-informed medicine is promising but not yet clinically validated for most SNPs |
| **Population structure** | South Asia has high endogamy; community-specific variants may not generalize |
| **Heuristic only** | Use as heuristic, not deterministic: Genotype is not destiny; environment and behavior dominate |
