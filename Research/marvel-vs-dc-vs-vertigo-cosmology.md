## Which Is "Bigger"?

### By **Hierarchy Depth** → **Vertigo > DC Mainline > Marvel**
- Vertigo has 4 distinct transcendent layers above Creation
- DC Mainline has 3 (Presence/Overvoid/Darkness)
- Marvel has 2 (OAA/Tribunal)

### By **Conceptual Scope** → **Vertigo > Marvel > DC Mainline**
- Vertigo's Endless are **ontologically fundamental** — Death *is* death, not an entity that manages it
- Marvel's Abstracts are functional embodiments — Eternity *is* the universe's time
- DC's Monitors are narrative observers, not concept-bound

### By **Multiverse Scale** → **Marvel (Omniverse) ≥ DC Mainline (Infinite + Dark) > Vertigo (Undefined)**
- Marvel explicitly includes *all* fiction via Omniverse
- DC has structured 52 + Dark + infinite past iterations
- Vertigo's multiverse is thematic, not quantified

### By **Feats at Top Tier** → **Marvel OAA = Vertigo Unknowable > DC Presence**
- OAA and Unknowable are both "writer/God beyond the story"
- DC Presence operates *within* the cosmology more often

## Complexity Verdict

**Most Complex: Vertigo**
- Theological + metaphysical + narrative layers
- Endless are unique in fiction: concepts that *are* the concept
- Unresolved relationship between Time/Night and Presence

**Most Structured: DC Mainline**
- Clear geometric mapping (Orrery, Spheres, Walls)
- Defined vibrational physics
- Explicit Monitor hierarchy

**Most Expansive: Marvel**
- Omniverse explicitly contains everything
- Branching timeline model allows infinite divergence
- Abstract Entity system covers every fundamental force

## Summary

| Winner | Category |
|--------|----------|
| **Vertigo** | Hierarchical depth, conceptual sophistication, theological complexity |
| **Marvel** | Raw scale (Omniverse), branching complexity, top-tier equality |

**Overall:** Vertigo > Marvel > DC Mainline for **conceptual complexity**. Marvel ≥ DC Mainline for **scale**. Vertigo's cosmology is fundamentally **different in kind** — it's a theology, not a physics.
