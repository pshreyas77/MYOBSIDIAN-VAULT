# Correction Note

> **Reference:** Genetica — https://doi.org/10.1007/s10709-018-0036-9
> **Corrections:** Corrects the molecular dating section of the main paper

## What Was Corrected

The original molecular dating section contained a calculation error in the second sentence of the **'Molecular dating'** section regarding the first mutation rate (stated as 1.26×10⁻⁸ should have been applied differently).

## Impact on Main Findings

The correction affects the **mutation rate calibration** used for coalescence age estimates. The novel subclade dating (~8.5 ± 5.6 kya for HV14a1b) may be affected. However, the overall phylogenetic structure and novel subclade identification remain valid.

**Action:** If citing specific dates from this paper for your Sanskritization project, note the correction and cross-check against the corrected calculation in future revisions or follow-up studies.
