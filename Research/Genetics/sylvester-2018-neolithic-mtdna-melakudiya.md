# Neolithic Phylogenetic Continuity in Southern Indian Tribe

> **Authors:** Charles Sylvester, Mysore Siddaiah Krishna, Jaya Sankar Rao, Adimoolam Chandrasekar
> **Published:** Genetica, 2018 (Springer Nature)
> **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s10709-018-0030-2

---

## Core Finding

**113 Melakudiya tribe individuals** (Dravidian-speaking, Kodagu district, Karnataka) were sequenced for complete mitochondrial DNA genomes.

Key findings:
- **46 of 113** individuals carried West Eurasian haplogroups **HV14** and **U7**
- Two **novel subclades** identified: **HV14a1b** and **HV14a1b1**
- U7 sequences classified under **U7a3a1a2*** (India-specific)
- Trace genetic link between Melakudiya and **Iranian Plateau, South Caucasus, and Central Asia**

## Key Genetic Dates

| Haplogroup | Coalescence Age |
|---|---|
| HV14 (in Melakudiya) | **~16.1 ± 4.3 kya** (Late Upper Paleolithic) |
| U7a3a1a2* | **~13.4 ± 5.6 kya** (Late Glacial) |
| Novel subclade HV14a1b | **~8.5 ± 5.6 kya** (Neolithic) |

## Why This Matters for Your Research

1. **Dravidian Origins:** The study confirms genetic continuity between South Indian tribal populations and West Eurasian (Iranian Plateau) lineages dating to the Neolithic — supporting the Dravidian language spread via demic diffusion from Elam province (SW Iran)

2. **Pre-Aryan Ancestry:** The Melakudiya are a tribal Dravidian-speaking group. Their maternal lineages trace back ~16kya, predating the Indo-Aryan migrations (~3.5-4 kya). This supports the ANI/ASI model where ASI (Ancestral South Indian) is largely indigenous

3. **Sanskritization Context:** West Eurasian haplogroups are found in higher-caste groups (Bamshad et al. 1998, 2001) — but this study shows the same haplogroups in tribal populations, suggesting either (a) pre-caste gene flow or (b) upward caste mobility over millennia

4. **IVC Connection:** The Iranian Plateau genetic link supports the McAlpin/SRenfrew hypothesis that proto-Dravidian originated in Elam province and spread with Neolithic agriculturalists

## Critical Context for Your Project

> **Evidence Grade:** Strong — full mitogenome sequencing (not just HVRI), novel clades, published in peer-reviewed journal

> **Confidence:** Settled for maternal lineage dating; moderate for demographic interpretation

> **Caveat:** Only maternal (mtDNA) — does not speak to paternal or autosomal lineages. The story on the Y-chromosome and autosomes may differ significantly.
