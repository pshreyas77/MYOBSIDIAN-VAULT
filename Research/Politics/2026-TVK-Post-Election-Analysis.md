## 1. Executive Summary

Your vault frames TVK as an upcoming 2026 variable. **The election has already occurred.** TVK won 108 of 234 seats (34.92% vote share), becoming the single largest party in a hung assembly — the first non-Dravidian party to achieve this since 1967. Vijay became Chief Minister on 8 May 2026 after securing external support from CPI, CPI(M), VCK, and IUML, who exited the DMK-led SPA to back an "NDA-free government."

## 3. The BJP Factor: A Catastrophic Failure

The BJP-AIADMK alliance (NDA) won only 53 seats total (AIADMK 47, BJP 1). BJP's vote share was negligible. The southern strategy in Tamil Nadu collapsed because:

- **TVK absorbed the anti-incumbency vote** that BJP hoped to capture
- **Hindi-Hindu-Hindustan framing remains electorally toxic in TN**
- **AIADMK is a declining vehicle** — fractured by EPS, OPS, and Sasikala factions

---

## 4. Governance Reality: Minority Government

TVK governs without a majority. The Left parties provide external support but retain SPA membership. This creates structural instability:

| Challenge | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Centre-State coordination** | Vijay campaigned as anti-BJP; now requires Central cooperation for welfare funding |
| **Coalition management** | CPI/CPI(M) will demand policy concessions on labor and welfare |
| **Cadre deficit** | TVK is a digital-mobilization party, not a cadre-based organization. Implementing welfare schemes requires bureaucratic infrastructure TVK lacks |
