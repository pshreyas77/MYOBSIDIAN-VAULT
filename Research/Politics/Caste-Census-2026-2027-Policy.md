# Caste Census 2026/2027 — Policy Deep Dive

**For future Claude:** Historic policy shift — Cabinet approved caste enumeration in Census (April 2025). First full caste census since 1931. Your vault needs this for the Dravidian Graph (caste coalition data layer) and politics MOC.

## 1. Historic Policy Shift

On **30 April 2025**, the Cabinet Committee on Political Affairs approved caste enumeration in the upcoming Census. This is the first full caste census since 1931. The Census was delayed from 2021 to 2027 due to COVID-19.

## 3. Methodological Innovations

- **Fully digital**: Mobile app enumeration + online self-enumeration portal
- **Standardized Code Directory**: Central OBC list (2,650 communities) + SC list (1,170) + ST list (890) merged with state lists to prevent SECC 2011's 46.7 lakh caste-name chaos
- **AI-assisted verification**: Real-time validation checks
- **Privacy safeguards**: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 compliance; anonymized publication
- **35 lakh field functionaries** to be trained

---

## 4. Why SECC 2011 Failed

The Socio-Economic and Caste Census 2011 collected data but **never released caste data**:

- 46.7 lakh caste entries due to open-ended self-reporting (people wrote sub-castes, surnames, clan names)
- 8 crore+ errors in classification
- Data handed to Panagariya Expert Group; report never made public
- Only SC/ST household data released by Rural Development Ministry

## 5. Legal Framework

| Instrument | Status |
|------------|--------|
| **Census Act 1948** | Currently only mandates SC/ST enumeration; amendment required for OBC inclusion (under discussion) |
| **Article 340** | Permits identification of backward classes |
| **Judicial mandate** | Indra Sawhney (1992), M. Nagaraj (2006), Jaishri Patil (2021), State of Punjab v. Davinder Singh (2024) — all require updated empirical data for reservation policies |

---

## 6. Policy Implications

| Area | Impact |
|------|--------|
| **OBC Sub-categorization** | Rohini Commission (2017) recommendations can be implemented with empirical data |
| **SC/ST Sub-classification** | Supreme Court permitted states to sub-classify within SC/ST quotas (2024); census data enables this |
| **Reservation Cap Debate** | Fresh data may fuel demands to revisit 50% cap (Indra Sawhney) |
| **Delimitation** | Census 2027 data will be used for Lok Sabha seat redistribution and 33% women's reservation |

## 8. Risks & Concerns

- **Reinforcing caste identities**: Critics argue it legitimizes divisions
- **Political exploitation**: Vote-bank politics, "competitive backwardness"
- **Misclassification**: Fluid caste identities, surname confusion, prestige claims
- **Data integrity**: Without robust verification, may repeat SECC 2011 failures
