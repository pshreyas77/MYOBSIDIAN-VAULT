## 2. Why It Failed in Tamil Nadu

|--------|----------|

## 3. Karnataka Contrast: The Success Model

BJP has won Karnataka state elections (2018, 2023) through:

| Factor | Karnataka Reality | TN Absence |
|--------|-------------------|------------|
| **Communal polarization** | Hijab row, halal, love jihad | No equivalent space (TVK occupies anti-minority space without Hindi baggage) |
| **Community leader** | BS Yediyurappa (Lingayat) | No equivalent |
| **Coastal radicalization** | RSS stronghold since 1990s | No coastal radicalization in TN |
| **Caste arithmetic** | Lingayat + Vokkaliga fragmentation | TN caste landscape is Dravidian-party captured |

**TN lacks ALL these conditions.**
