# Modern Rationalist Organizations — Current Status & Risk Assessment

## 1. Federation of Indian Rationalist Associations (FIRA)

- **Status**: Active but fragmented; umbrella body with state-level affiliates
- **Activities**: National Science Festival events, anti-superstition campaigns, media interventions
- **Challenge**: No unified national leadership; funding constraints; regional variation in activity

---

## 2. Maharashtra Andhashraddha Nirmoolan Samiti (MANS)

- **Founded**: 1989 by Dr. Narendra Dabholkar
- **Current leadership**: Hamid Dabholkar (son)
- **Key achievement**: Maharashtra Anti-Superstition and Black Magic Act (2013) — passed posthumously after Dabholkar's assassination
- **Status**: Active but under severe pressure; legal cases filed by Hindutva groups against volunteers; police protection required for events

---

## 3. Sanal Edamaruku & Indian Rationalist Association

- **Status**: President in exile in Finland since 2012
- **Reason**: Blasphemy charges (IPC 295A) for exposing a Catholic "miracle" (water dripping from crucifix in Mumbai) as capillary action from a leaky toilet
- **Current risk**: Cannot return to India; arrest warrant remains active; extradition risk if he travels to certain countries
