## Supplement Tiers

### Tier A — Strong Evidence (Recommended)
From [[Research/2026-06-21 — Supplement Evidence Audit & Master List]]:
- **Whey Protein** — Muscle protein synthesis, recovery
- **Creatine Monohydrate** — Strength, cognitive benefits
- **Caffeine** — Performance, fat oxidation
- **Omega-3 (Fish Oil)** — Inflammation, cardiovascular
- **Vitamin D3** — Deficiency correction (India: common)
- **Berberine** — Blood glucose, insulin sensitivity *(contradiction resolved in audit)*

### Tier B — Moderate Evidence
- Ashwagandha — Stress, testosterone
- Beta-Alanine — Muscular endurance
- Citrulline Malate — Blood flow, pump

### Tier C — Emerging / Context-Dependent
- Probiotics — Gut health
- Magnesium — Sleep, recovery

### Tier D — Discard / Low Evidence
- BCAAs — Redundant with adequate protein
- Fat burners — Minimal evidence, high risk
