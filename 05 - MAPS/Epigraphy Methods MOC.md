**For future Claude:** Your evidence grading depends on this. IVC seals are undeciphered — do not treat them as "writing" in Tier A. They are Tier C at best. Connects to your "hard physical evidence" tier in Aryan migration synthesis.

## 1. South Asian Epigraphic Corpora

| Corpus | Coverage | Language | Digital Access |
|--------|----------|----------|----------------|
| **CII (Corpus Inscriptionum Indicarum)** | Pan-India; Ashokan to Gupta | Prakrit, Sanskrit, Tamil | Partial (ASI); volumes I–VII |
| **EI (Epigraphia Indica)** | Annual journal, 1892–present | Multiple | Some volumes digitized; not comprehensive |
| **SII (South Indian Inscriptions)** | Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam | Dravidian + Sanskrit | ASI website (incomplete) |
| **ARE (Annual Reports on Indian Epigraphy)** | 1887–present | Multiple | Microfilm at ASI; limited digital |
| **CII Madras** | Madras Presidency | Tamil, Telugu | Partial |

---

## 2. Dating Methods

| Method | Reliability | Application |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **Paleography** | Medium | Script style dating; requires comparative corpus |
| **Paleoclimatology** | Medium | Varve chronology, dendrochronology (rare in India) |
| **Radiocarbon** | High (if direct) | Direct dating of organic material in inscriptions |
| **Regnal year correlation** | High | When inscription mentions king's regnal year, cross-check with astronomical data |
| **Stratigraphy** | Medium | Archaeological context; terminus post quem |

## 3. Digital Epigraphy

| Technique | Description | Projects Using It |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **TEI/EpiDoc** | XML encoding standard for inscriptions | Dharma project (EFEO) |
| **RTI (Reflectance Transformation Imaging)** | Digital technique to reveal erased/worn text | IITs, ASI |
| **3D Scanning** | Structured light scanning for inscriptions | IITs, ASI |
| **Corpus search** | Digital Sanskrit corpus (DCS), SARIT, GRETIL for textual parallels | — |

## 4. Evidence Grading for Your Vault

| Tier | Evidence Type | Examples |
|------|--------------|----------|
| **A** | Contemporary inscription, direct date, multiple readings | Ashokan Brahmi inscriptions; Mitanni tablets |
| **B** | Later inscription, secure date, corroborated | Gupta inscriptions; copper plates |
| **C** | Textual reference in later work, no independent corroboration | Puranic king lists; Rajatarangiṇī |
| **D** | Philological reconstruction, no material attestation | Rigvedic dating; PIE reconstruction |

> **CRITICAL NOTE**: IVC seals are undeciphered. Do not treat them as "writing" in Tier A. They are **Tier C at best** (symbols with possible semantic content, no decipherment).
