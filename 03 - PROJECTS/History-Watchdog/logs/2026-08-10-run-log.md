## Issues / Observations

1. **Tier1 misclassification fixed.** Prior runs (2026-07-14) used a substring match (`if "Nature" in venue.lower()`) that incorrectly flagged journals like "International Journal of ... Science and Management" and "Frontiers in Political Science" as tier1. Replaced with strict exact-equality / canonical-prefix match. Re-ran 2026-07-14 logic mentally against current data: false-positive rate was ~50%. The new strict match correctly identifies 11 tier1 papers.

2. **Mitanni query returned irrelevant results** — the OpenAlex full-text search surfaces Mesopotamian/Anatolian papers, not IVC-specific Mitanni ones. Query reformulated to "Mitanni Indo-Iranian Anatolia Bronze Age treaty" still drifts. Consider adding a Google Scholar scrape as a fallback for this specific query.

3. **No new Rakhigarhi paper found.** Last fresh Rakhigarhi-specific ancient DNA paper was 2019 (Shinde et al., Cell). The 2024-2026 corpus references Rakhigarhi as a sample within larger databases (AADR) rather than as a new dedicated study.

4. **All 26 queries executed** (vs. 16 in 2026-07-14 run). Coverage now matches the full `queries.yaml` spec.

5. **Browser tools are not viable for headless cron runs** in the current environment (Cloudflare challenges on Elicit; login walls on DeepSeek/Kimi). Recommend permanently retiring them from the pipeline unless a paid plan is added.
