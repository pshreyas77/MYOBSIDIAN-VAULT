## Tier 1: Kitchen Staples (Cheapest, Strongest Evidence)

### Psyllium Husk (Isabgol) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **What:** Natural soluble fiber, forms gel in stomach  
- **How:** Delays gastric emptying, generates satiety, blocks blood sugar spikes  
- **Dose:** 5–10g (2 tsp) in water, 30 min before breakfast and dinner  
- **Cost:** Extremely cheap in India  
- **Evidence: Strong** — clinically proven for weight loss and glucose control  

### Vinegar (Apple Cider / White) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **What:** Acetic acid from fermented carbohydrates  
- **How:** Blocks starch breakdown, slows gastric emptying, activates AMPK (fat clearing)  
- **Dose:** 10–15 mL diluted in water, directly before meals  
- **Cost:** Very cheap  
- **Evidence: Strong** — RCTs show significant BMI and body fat reduction  

### Cinnamon (Dalchini) ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **What:** Common kitchen spice  
- **How:** Mimics insulin effects, suppresses sugar production in liver  
- **Dose:** 1.5g–6g daily (1/2 to 2 tsp), add to morning coffee  
- **Cost:** Very cheap  
- **Evidence: Moderate/Strong** — meta-analyses confirm sugar/lipid improvements  

### Ginger Powder (Sonth) ⭐⭐⭐
- **What:** Dried ginger root  
- **How:** Thermogenesis (calorie burning via body heat), insulin sensitizer  
- **Dose:** 1–2g daily (1/2 tsp), morning or before meals  
- **Cost:** Very cheap  
- **Evidence: Moderate** — RCTs show fat mass and waist reduction  

### Hibiscus Tea (Gudhal) ⭐⭐⭐
- **What:** Tea from dried hibiscus calyces  
- **How:** Blocks carb absorption enzyme, downregulates new fat cell formation  
- **Dose:** 5g loose leaf as tea, morning (detox) or evening (lipid control)  
- **Cost:** Cheap  
- **Evidence: Moderate** — human trials confirm BMI and liver fat reduction  

### Fenugreek (Methi) ⭐⭐⭐
- **How:** Keeps blood sugar in check, complementary therapy  
- **Dose:** Traditional use; consult for specific extracts  
- **Cost:** Very cheap  
- **Evidence: Moderate**  

### Nigella Sativa (Kalonji) ⭐⭐⭐
- **How:** High in thymoquinone, ramps insulin secretion, moves sugar out of blood  
- **Dose:** 0.7–2g daily (oil or powder)  
- **Cost:** Cheap  
- **Evidence: Moderate**

## Tier 2: Bulk Raw Supplements (Science-Backed, Low Cost)

### Taurine ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **How:** Suppresses fat accumulation genes, increases fat-burning hormone ATGL, prevents muscle damage  
- **Dose:** 3g daily (split: 1.5g pre-workout + 1.5g post-workout)  
- **Cost:** Cheap as bulk powder  
- **Evidence: Moderate/Strong** — meta-analyses show consistent results in overweight males  

### Creatine Monohydrate ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **How:** Recycles muscle energy, increases reps → builds lean mass, prevents muscle loss during fat loss  
- **Dose:** 5g daily, anytime  
- **Cost:** Affordable, widely available in India  
- **Evidence: Strong** — extremely well-studied  

### Glycine ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **How:** Lowers core body temperature for restorative sleep, precursor for glutathione (antioxidant)  
- **Dose:** 3–8g, 1 hour before sleep  
- **Cost:** Cheap as bulk powder  
- **Evidence: Moderate/Strong**  

### Berberine (Daruharidra) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **How:** Activates AMPK (metabolic master switch), improves insulin receptor sensitivity  
- **Dose:** 500mg, 2–3 times daily before meals  
- **Cost:** Moderate — Indian herb extract available  
- **Evidence: Strong** — effectiveness compared to metformin  

### Whey Protein ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **How:** Preserves muscle during fat loss, activates muscle protein synthesis via leucine  
- **Dose:** 25–40g post-workout  
- **Cost:** Moderately expensive but essential if food protein is low  
- **Evidence: Strong**  

### Caffeine (Coffee/Chai) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **How:** Boosts metabolic rate, burns fat for fuel via adrenaline  
- **Dose:** Up to 400mg/day, 60 min pre-exercise  
- **Cost:** Free if using standard coffee/chai  
- **Evidence: Strong**

## Tier 3: Zero-Cost Natural Strategies

### NEAT (Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis)
- Stand while working, pace during calls, take stairs  
- Can burn up to 2,000 extra calories without gym  
- **10–15 min brisk walk after every major meal** — clears glucose directly into muscle  

### Intermittent Fasting (16:8)
- Eating window: 10 AM to 6 PM (early window)  
- Early Time-Restricted Feeding superior for insulin sensitivity and blood pressure  
- Front-load carbs earlier in day, restrict in evening meals  

### Meal Sequencing ("Fiber First")
1. **Fiber first** — vegetables (spinach, broccoli, okra) → creates gel barrier, slows sugar absorption  
2. **Protein + fats second** — stimulates GLP-1, PYY (fullness hormones)  
3. **Carbs last** — roti, lentils, brown rice at end  
4. Wait 10–15 min between phases for max effect  
5. Post-meal glucose spikes reduced by **30–40%**  

### Cold Exposure
- Cold showers or 1–3 min uncomfortable cold exposure  
- Activates brown fat, which burns calories to generate heat  

### Morning Sunlight
- 45+ min outdoors upon waking  
- Resets circadian rhythm, enhances baseline fat oxidation
