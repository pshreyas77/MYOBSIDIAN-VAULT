## 🔑 Key Themes to Explore

### 1. Constitutional Framework
- **Article 82** - Delimitation after each Census
- **Article 170** - State legislative assembly delimitation
- **84th Amendment (2001)** - Froze delimitation till first Census after 2026
- **87th Amendment (2003)** - Minor adjustments allowed

### 2. Current Status (as of 2026)
- **Frozen since 1976** (based on 1971 Census)
- **Population asymmetry:** North grew faster than South
- **Representation gap:** Southern states penalized for population control success

### 3. Political Implications
| Dimension | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| **Lok Sabha seats** | North gains, South loses |
| **Federal finance** | Tax devolution affected |
| **State politics** | Assembly seat redistribution |
| **Minority representation** | Reserved constituency changes |

### 4. Key Debates
- **One Nation, One Election** linkage
- **Women's Reservation Bill** (33% quota) timing
- **Federalism vs Population parity**
- **Census delay** (2021 Census postponed)
