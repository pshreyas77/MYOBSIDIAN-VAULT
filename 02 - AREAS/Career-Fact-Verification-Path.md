## Portfolio Projects (3-Part Roadmap)

Build in order. Zero code needed. Takes ~2 weeks total.

### Project 1 — Public Fact-Check Writeup
**Do first. Fastest win. Takes 1 evening.**

Pick 1 claim you already researched (e.g. TN 2026 election result, Mitanni treaty date, etc). Write short piece:

1. Claim stated plainly
2. Sources checked (list, with links)
3. Verdict: true / false / partially true / outdated
4. Why — 3-4 lines

Post on Medium or LinkedIn. This = your writing sample.

### Project 2 — Political/Current-Affairs Tracker
**Ongoing. Shows sustained discipline, not one-off.**

Pick ongoing topic — TGEAPCET counselling, state election results, JPC process. Track over 2-3 weeks:

1. Log claims found in news
2. Verify each against official source / primary doc
3. Note corrections when claim changes or turns false

Publish as running doc (Notion / Google Doc, public link) or short weekly LinkedIn posts.

### Case Study 2: Physical Hatha Yoga (Asanas) — Ancient vs Medieval

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Old claim (wrong)** | Physical Hatha Yoga (asana practice) = ancient Vedic/pre-Vedic tradition, thousands of years old |
| **Corrected claim** | **Physical Hatha Yoga (asana system) = medieval, ~11th century CE.** Earliest systematic text: **Amritasiddhi** (c. 11th CE, Buddhist/Tantric milieu). Seated meditation (dhyana) may have pre-Aryan roots (contested IVC seal), but postural yoga is a medieval Tantric/Buddhist synthesis. |
| **Primary source proving correction** | **Amritasiddhi** (Sanskrit manuscript, c. 11th CE) — earliest text teaching physical asanas, bindu conservation, and Hatha techniques. Later systematized in **Hathapradipika** (15th CE, Svatmarama). No pre-10th century text describes physical postures as yoga practice. |
| **Date corrected** | 2025-06 (during Yoga/Ayurveda/Siddha synthesis research) |
| **Why this matters** | Debunks "yoga is purely Vedic/5000 years old" nationalist claim. Separates meditative asceticism (probable pre-Aryan) from physical postural yoga (medieval Tantric). |

### Case Study 3: Rakhigarhi aDNA — IVC Had Zero Steppe Ancestry

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Old claim (wrong)** | Indus Valley Civilization = Aryan/Vedic; IVC people had Steppe pastoralist ancestry (Out-of-India Theory claim) |
| **Corrected claim** | **IVC = zero Steppe ancestry.** The Rakhigarhi individual (I6332, female, ~2500 BCE) carries **only AASI + Iranian farmer ancestry**. Steppe ancestry enters South Asia **after** IVC decline (2000–1500 BCE). |
| **Primary source proving correction** | **Shinde et al. 2019, *Cell* ("An Ancient Harappan Genome...")** — sequenced first IVC genome (Rakhigarhi, Haryana). **Narasimhan et al. 2019, *Science* ("The Formation of Human Populations in South and Central Asia")** — 523 ancient genomes confirming Steppe arrival post-IVC. |
| **Date corrected** | 2025-06 (during Aryan Migration synthesis — 54 research passes) |
| **Why this matters** | Single strongest genetic falsification of Out-of-India Theory. If IVC = Aryan homeland, Rakhigarhi genome should have Steppe ancestry. It has none. |

### Case Study 4: Kikkuli Manual CTH 284 — Indo-Aryan Numerals More Archaic Than Rigveda

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Old claim (wrong)** | Rigveda preserves the oldest Indo-Aryan numerals; Sanskrit is the source |
| **Corrected claim** | **Kikkuli Horse Training Manual (CTH 284, ~1345 BCE, Hittite cuneiform, Bogazköy) preserves MORE ARCHAIC Indo-Aryan numerals than Rigveda:** *aika* (1), *tera* (3), *panza* (5), *satta* (7), *nawa* (9) — showing *aika* vs Vedic *eka* (diphthong loss), *panza* vs *panca* (retention of *z*). |
| **Primary source proving correction** | **Kikkuli tablet CTH 284** — Hittite cuneiform tablet from Bogazköy (Hattusa), Anatolia, ~1345 BCE. Published in Kammenhuber 1961, Starr 1939, Fournet 2010. Physical tablet exists; numerals are Indo-Aryan, not Iranian or Indo-European generic. |
| **Date corrected** | 2025-06 (during Mitanni/Kikkuli evidence tier research) |
| **Why this matters** | Proves Indo-Aryan speech existed outside India by 1345 BCE with archaic features LOST in Rigveda. Migration is physically attested; Rigveda is later composition preserving evolved forms. |

### Case Study 5: Oldest Physical Rigveda Manuscript = 1040 CE (Not 1200 BCE)

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Old claim (wrong)** | Rigveda = ancient text from ~1200 BCE; "oldest religious text in the world" |
| **Corrected claim** | **Rigveda composition estimated ~1500–1200 BCE (linguistic/astronomical inference), but OLDEST PHYSICAL MANUSCRIPT = 1040 CE (Nepal, palm leaf).** 2,200-year gap between composition estimate and physical evidence. Same problem for Avesta (oldest MS = 13th–14th CE). |
| **Primary source proving correction** | **Nepal-German Manuscript Preservation Project** — Rigveda MS **B 30/31** (Nepal, palm leaf, dated 1040 CE, colophon). Catalogued in *Catalogue of Sanskrit Manuscripts in Nepal* (NGMPP). No older physical Rigveda manuscript exists anywhere. |
| **Date corrected** | 2025-06 (during Written Philosophy Chronology research — strict physical MS evidence only) |
| **Why this matters** | Distinguishes **composition date** (reconstructed, indirect) from **physical attestation** (direct evidence). Egypt/Sumer win on physical evidence: Ptahhotep (Papyrus Prisse, ~2390 BCE), Shuruppak tablets (~2600 BCE). India's textual tradition is oral-transmitted; physical MSS are late. |
