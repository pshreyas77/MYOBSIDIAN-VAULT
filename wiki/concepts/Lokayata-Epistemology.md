# Lokayata Epistemology — Perception vs Inference Debate

## The Core Correction

The common claim that "Cārvākas rejected inference (anumāna) entirely" is a **Brahminical/Buddhist caricature** from opponent texts (Madhava, Śāntarakṣita, Kumārila). Current scholarship (Bhattacharya, Franco, Preisendanz) distinguishes three positions:

---

## 1. Mainstream Cārvāka: Perception-Only for Metaphysics

| Domain | Epistemological Status | Reasoning |
|--------|------------------------|-----------|
| **Metaphysical claims** (afterlife, god, soul, karma) | **Inference rejected** | Such inferences lack grounding in verifiable perception (pratyakṣa) |
| **Mundane causation** (fire from smoke, seed from sprout) | **Inference accepted** | Directly verifiable through repeated perception |
| **Scriptural authority (śabda)** | **Rejected entirely** | Not a valid pramāṇa; Vedas are human compositions |

**Key principle**: Inference is valid only when the invariable concomitance (vyāpti) between hetu (reason) and sādhya (probandum) is established through perception.

---

## 2. Jayarāśi's Radical Skepticism (Tattvopaplavasiṃha)

| Position | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Target** | ALL pramāṇas including perception |
| **Method** | Shows each pramāṇa fails its own criteria (e.g., perception cannot perceive its own validity) |
| **Classification** | Skeptic (tattvopaplavasiṃha = "upsetting of principles"), not materialist |
| **Survival reason** | Refuted by Buddhists AND Hindus — seen as threat to ALL epistemological systems |

**Critical**: Jayarāśi ≠ Cārvāka. He is a *skeptic* who uses Cārvāka arguments to undermine all knowledge claims.

---

## 3. Opponent Caricature (Madhava, Śāntarakṣita, Kumārila)

| Source | Claim | Reality |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Madhava (Sarvadarśanasaṅgraha) | Cārvākas accept only perception; reject all inference | Hostile summary; conflates Jayarāśi with Cārvāka |
| Śāntarakṣita (Tattvasaṅgraha) | Refutes "perception-only" position | Accurate on Jayarāśi; misattributes to Cārvāka |
| Kumārila (Ślokavārttika) | Cārvāka epistemology is self-refuting | Polemical; targets straw man |

## 4. Why This Matters for Your Vault

Your `wiki/concepts/Indian Atheism, Rationalism, and Anti-Caste Struggle.md` likely frames Cārvāka as "rejected inference." **This is incorrect.** The correct framing:

> "Cārvākas accepted perception as primary and inference for empirically verifiable causation, but rejected inference for metaphysical claims and scriptural authority as a pramāṇa."

This distinction:
- Strengthens your "rationalist lineage" — Cārvākas were not anti-reason, they were anti-unverified-metaphysics
- Connects to modern scientific epistemology (verificationism)
- Distinguishes materialist Cārvāka from skeptical Jayarāśi
