# Ravidas

## Overview
**Dates**: 1450–1520 CE  
**Caste**: Chamar (leatherworker) — Dalit by birth, untouchable status  
**Tradition**: Nirguṇa Bhakti (formless divine through loving devotion)  
**Significance**: Only Dalit voice in Guru Granth Sahib with substantial presence (40 verses)

## Texts

| Text | Status | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| **Guru Granth Sahib** | 40 verses | Largest single collection |
| **Ravidassia Gutka** | Community scripture | |
| **Amritbani** | 2009 compilation | Ravidassia religion movement |

## Core Philosophy

| Tenet | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Begumpura** | Utopian vision: city without caste, tax, sorrow, or domination — **this-worldly moral ideal** |
| **Dignity of labor** | Elevated leatherwork as spiritual practice |
| **Anti-Brahminism** | Rejected priestly mediation, ritual purity, Vedic authority |
| **Theistic Nirguṇa** | Devotion to formless divine through loving surrender (bhakti), not Kabir's confrontational skepticism |

## Modern Political Significance

| Movement | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Ravidassia Separate Religion (2009)** | Broke from Sikhism after Vienna temple shooting (2009). Own scripture (Amritbani), symbol (Harr Nishaan), rituals. |
| **Dalit politics** | Claimed by BSP, Azad Samaj Party, and various Dalit cultural movements as foundational icon |
