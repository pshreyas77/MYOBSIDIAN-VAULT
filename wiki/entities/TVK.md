# TVK (Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam)

## Overview
**Founded**: 2024-02-02  
**Leader**: C. Joseph Vijay  
**Ideology**: Populist welfare, Tamil nationalism, anti-BJP, youth-centric  
**Status**: Ruling party (minority government) as of May 2026  

## 2026 Election Result
- **Seats**: 108/234 (single largest party, hung assembly)
- **Vote Share**: 34.92%
- **Government**: TVK + external support (CPI, CPI(M), VCK, IUML)
- **CM**: Vijay sworn in 8 May 2026

## Key Policies
- Women-centric welfare: ₹2,500/month
- Drug-free Tamil Nadu
- Student loan waivers
- Film industry support

## Caste Strategy
Explicitly avoided caste-based mobilization; relied on star power + urban youth + women

## Film-Politics Precedent
MGR (1977) — but TVK is digital-native, not cadre-based

## Critical Risks
- Coalition instability (minority government)
- Cadre deficit (no pre-existing organizational infrastructure)
- Centre-State friction (anti-BJP stance vs need for Central funding)
