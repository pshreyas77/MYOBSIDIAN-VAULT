# Bakhtiyar-Khalji

## Overview
**Dates**: Late 12th – early 13th c. CE (died ~1206)  
**Role**: Turkic general under Muhammad of Ghor; governor of Bengal  
**Significance**: Destroyed Nalanda, Vikramashila, Odantapuri (1193–1203) — terminal accelerator for Buddhism in Bihar

## Key Events

| Year | Event | Significance |
|------|-------|--------------|
| ~1193 | Conquest of Bihar | Overruns Buddhist heartland |
| ~1197 | Destruction of Nalanda | Library burned for months; monks killed/fled |
| ~1200 | Destruction of Vikramashila | Last major eastern Indian monastic university |
| ~1203 | Destruction of Odantapuri | Final blow to institutional Buddhism in Bihar |
| ~1206 | Death | Killed by own officer (Ali Mardan) |

## Historical Role in Buddhism's Decline

| Aspect | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **Primary cause?** | NO — Buddhism already marginal in North India by 1000 CE |
| **Regional impact** | Terminal **because Buddhism was still institutionally strong in Bihar/Bengal** |
| **Elsewhere** | North India: already declined; South India: never dominant; Kashmir: Śaiva ascendancy; Northwest: lost to Islam earlier |
| **Consensus** | "Final blow" in eastern India; not pan-Indian cause |
