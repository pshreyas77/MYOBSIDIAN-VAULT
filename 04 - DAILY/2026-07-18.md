## Key Findings for Vault

### Justice Party → Dravidar Kazhagam → DMK Lineage
1. **1916**: Justice Party founded at Kumbakonam (Victoria Public Hall) by Chetty, Nair, Mudaliar
2. **1920–1937**: Governed Madras Presidency 13 of 17 years under Dyarchy; passed Communal G.O.s (first caste reservation in India)
3. **1935–1944**: Periyar joins (1935), elected President 1938/1939 from Vellore Jail; transforms elite caste-association → radical mass movement
4. **1944**: Salem Conference — renamed **Dravidar Kazhagam**, withdrew from elections, pure social reform
5. **1949**: Annadurai splits to form DMK (electoral politics)
6. **1967**: DMK wins Madras State — Dravidian era begins

### Critical Nuance
- **Periyar never founded a political party** — he led Justice Party (1938–44), transformed it to DK, opposed DMK split
- **Communal G.O. = direct ancestor of Article 16(4)** and post-independence reservation
- **Dalits often marginalized** in DK/DMK despite anti-caste rhetoric — graded inequality reproduced
