## 🔴 Headline: Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) "Chalo Sansad" March

A youth-led protest movement demanding Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan's resignation attempted today's **"Chalo Sansad"** (March to Parliament) march on Day 1 of the Monsoon Session. Delhi Police used tear gas and lathicharge; 10,000-20,000 marched; mass detentions occurred.

> **Important note about the name:** "CJP" here is a **satirical protest movement** named after CJI Surya Kant's "cockroach/parasite" remark. It is **NOT** Teesta Setalvad's "Citizens for Justice and Peace" organisation (different entity, same acronym).
